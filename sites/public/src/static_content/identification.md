#### Tenant Selection and Required Documentation

Each property has its own tenant selection process. In most cases, you won't need any supporting documentation to complete the initial application on this website. However, you should begin gathering the documentation you'll need if your application is selected for review.

Under the **Additional Information** section of the property listing, there may be a **Required Documents** text box that lists which documents you’ll need. Don't wait to get started.

---

#### Income

If you’re selected to move forward in the selection process, you will be asked to provide proof of household income.

This applies to all sources of income for each member of your household who is **18 years of age or older**.

Supporting documentation can include:

- Welfare, veteran benefits, or Social Security letter  
- Rental assistance voucher  
- Recent pay stubs from your current employer  
- Self-employment proof  
- Alimony documentation  
- Current pension or retirement income  

---

#### Assets

This applies to all sources of assets, including income-related assets, for each member of your household who is **18 years of age or older**.

Assets can include:

- Checking account statements  
- Savings account statements  
- Real property  
- Investment accounts  

---

#### Additional Information

Refer to the property listing or the property's own application requirements for more information about the financial documents that will be required to determine eligibility.
